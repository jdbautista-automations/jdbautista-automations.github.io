Project Overview

Natural conversation, guarded transactions.

Amihan is an AI-powered booking and payment-operations MVP for tourism businesses. It connects a real WhatsApp Business number to n8n, OpenAI, and Airtable so guest messages can become structured business records and clear staff actions.

Tourism teams often handle inquiries, availability, reservations, payment instructions, receipt review, and confirmations manually across chat and spreadsheets. That creates recurring risks: slow responses, duplicate reservations, oversold inventory, lost conversational context, and payment confirmation without controlled human review.

Amihan separates interpretation from authority. OpenAI interprets language; n8n and Airtable remain authoritative for availability, booking, payment, and review state.

Accurate current status The core transaction journey is verified in a recorded real-device demonstration. Permanent production hosting and one post-completion conversation-routing correction remain on the roadmap.

System Architecture

AI interprets. Operational records decide.

The guest-facing workflow handles conversation and transaction orchestration. A separate Airtable-triggered admin workflow handles employee approval or rejection.

01 · Meta

WhatsApp webhook

Inbound messages from a real WhatsApp user arrive through Meta’s webhook.

02 · Resolve

Guest and conversation

n8n normalizes the message and loads or creates the linked guest and conversation.

03 · Understand

Intent and details

OpenAI extracts the guest’s intent and relevant booking information from natural language.

04 · Verify

Airtable authority

Offerings, availability, bookings, guests, settings, and conversation state are loaded.

05 · Decide

Deterministic branches

Rules control availability, reservation, payment instructions, and human handoff.

06 · Respond

Guest or employee action

Meta sends the reply, or the admin workflow waits for and processes employee review.

Verified Guest Journey

From “Is this available?” to confirmed booking.

The demonstration covered the full operational path, including duplicate protection and a human decision before payment state could be confirmed.

01 · INQUIRY

Availability in natural language

Amihan identifies the request, resolves the guest, checks Airtable, and replies with verified availability.

02 · RESERVATION

Pending booking creation

A final availability check reserves the required units and creates a linked Pending / Unpaid booking with a Booking ID.

03 · IDEMPOTENCY

Duplicate protection

Repeated booking requests resolve to the existing linked booking without creating another record or reserving more units.

04 · PAYMENT

Configured instructions

Business settings supply the method, account information, amount, and booking reference with demo-only warnings.

05 · HANDOFF

Payment-proof review

Proof changes the conversation to Payment Verification / Human Handoff while the booking stays Pending / Unpaid.

06 · DECISION

Employee approval or rejection

Approved becomes Confirmed / Deposit Paid; rejected stays Pending / Unpaid and returns the employee’s review note.

07 · REPEAT SAFETY

Idempotent review processing

A processed flag and state checks stop the same decision from changing records or notifying the guest twice.

What Testing Revealed

A conversational issue—not a transactional failure.

After the booking was confirmed, the guest sent a celebratory thank-you. The intent extractor reused earlier booking context and reopened the conversation as an availability check.

The important safety result was that the confirmed booking and payment state did not change, no duplicate booking was created, and no extra availability was reserved. The problem sits in post-completion conversation routing.

Designed v5.2 correction · not yet implemented A state-aware router will run before normal intent handling whenever the linked booking is already Confirmed / Deposit Paid.
  • Courtesy or acknowledgement: send a brief closing reply and keep the conversation completed.
  • Explicit new booking: open a new booking journey.
  • Support, modification, or cancellation: route to its workflow or a human.
  • Ambiguous message: ask one clarifying question without reopening availability.

Reusable Direction

A configurable tourism-operations template.

Offerings, rates, capacity, payment details, FAQs, and messaging live in Airtable so the automation can be adapted for tours, accommodations, and related booking businesses without hard-coding every business value.

Documented next steps:

  1. Implement and regression-test the v5.2 post-completion router.
  2. Retrieve and store receipt images or media metadata.
  3. Move from a Cloudflare Quick Tunnel to a named tunnel or VPS deployment.
  4. Add centralized error handling and Telegram operational alerts.
  5. Package onboarding fields and configuration for reuse across client businesses.
  6. Add stronger concurrency controls and evaluate Redis-backed queue mode when scale requires it.
