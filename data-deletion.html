User Data Deletion Instructions

Amihan Booking Bot Demo
Last updated: August 26, 2026

You may request deletion of personal information processed by JDBautista Automations through the Amihan Booking Bot Demo.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Send an email to jdbautista.automations@gmail.com .
  2. Use the subject: Amihan Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the WhatsApp number used with the demo, including its country code.
  4. Include your approximate interaction date and, if available, the related Booking ID or Conversation ID.

Do not include passwords, one-time PINs, payment credentials, government identification documents, or other sensitive information in your request.

Verification

We may request limited information to confirm that you control the WhatsApp number associated with the requested records. This helps prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Information Covered

Once verified, the request may cover information controlled by JDBautista Automations, including:

  • Guest profile and WhatsApp identifiers;
  • Conversation messages and summaries;
  • Demo booking and availability links;
  • Payment-review notes and status information; and
  • Related workflow records and testing logs.

Processing Time

Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days. We will send confirmation to the requesting email address when processing is complete.

Limitations

Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or establishing that a deletion request was completed. Information processed independently by third-party service providers may also be subject to their respective retention and privacy policies.
