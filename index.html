About Me

Customer Support → Automation & Operations

I’m Joshua Bautista, an automation builder based in the Philippines with nearly seven years of customer-support experience across collections, food delivery, telecommunications, sales, technical support, and e-commerce.

That background gives me a practical view of the work surrounding customer service: monitoring conversations, maintaining records, handling exceptions, coordinating handoffs, protecting service levels, and keeping customers informed.

I now combine that operational perspective with HighLevel, n8n, OpenAI, WhatsApp Cloud API, Airtable, Gmail, Google Sheets, Apps Script, JavaScript, Docker, Cloudflare, and API integrations.

Portfolio systems use fictional scenarios and synthetic data where appropriate. I distinguish portfolio verification from paid production deployment and document implementation boundaries rather than hiding them.
