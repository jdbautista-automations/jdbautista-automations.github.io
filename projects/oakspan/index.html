Why Oakspan Exists

A roofing inquiry is only useful when the next action is clear.

Oakspan Roofing & Exteriors is a fictional residential-roofing business created as a portfolio environment. It models the operational work behind a local contractor receiving inquiries from a website, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and live chat while coordinating inspections through one CRM.

The risk is not simply a missed message. A weak intake process can create duplicate opportunities, misroute an uncertain ZIP, treat an existing customer as a new sale, ignore a no-follow-up instruction, or allow an automated response to continue when a person should take over.

The system uses explicit fields, tags, branches, ownership rules, tasks, notifications, pipeline stages, and calendar events to keep each contact on a controlled path. Artificial intelligence is not given authority to invent an outcome or bypass a safeguard.

Deterministic control before automated persuasion Allowed cases progress automatically. Missing, uncertain, warranty, and low-confidence cases stop and route to human review with the reason preserved.

Automation Workflow

From inquiry to completed inspection.

Each interaction follows a controlled path designed to preserve lead context, prevent duplicate opportunities, and route uncertain cases to a human.

01 · CAPTURE

Bring every inquiry into one CRM

Website forms, live chat, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram DM enter the same operating environment with their source retained.

02 · ROUTE

Resolve service-area confidence

Property ZIP and inquiry details determine an approved route, a plausible DFW manual review, or a missing or unrecognized ZIP handoff.

03 · QUALIFY

Apply business rules consistently

Residential authority, requested service, primary intent, and follow-up permission decide qualification, protection, or human review.

04 · PROGRESS

Synchronize the inspection lifecycle

Opportunity stages follow qualification, calendar booking, and inspection completion while duplicate creation remains blocked.

Operational Safeguards

Automation that knows when to stop.

The workflows are built around explicit guardrails, not only happy-path actions. Every safeguard below was configured as an operational behavior inside HighLevel.

Duplicate control

Find before creating

Opportunity workflows search first and disallow multiple opportunities so repeat execution does not create another deal.

Consent control

Respect no-follow-up

A no-follow-up choice exits qualification, records a Not Qualified outcome, and applies opt-out protection.

Confidence control

Review uncertain cases

Missing authority, unknown service, incomplete permission, and ambiguous ZIP data generate a Needs Information route.

Human operations

Assign, task, and notify

Handoff contacts receive an owner, a dated review task, and an internal notification that opens the correct contact.

Source fidelity

Preserve the inquiry origin

Website, Messenger, Instagram, and calendar-origin contacts retain source context through downstream routing.

Cost boundary

Test without outbound spend

The verified workflow suite used no email actions and recorded $0.00 total spend during controlled testing.

Current Boundary

A portfolio system with honest production limits.

Oakspan is a synthetic demonstration, not a live contractor account. No real customer information, service request, payment, or roofing appointment should be submitted through the page.

A dedicated email-sending domain and WhatsApp channel are intentionally not presented as completed capabilities. The tested workflows contain no email-send action, and WhatsApp would require a separate business-number and account setup.

The original landing page was prototyped inside HighLevel. GitHub Pages provides the public demonstration layer while the embedded form, chat widget, CRM records, conversations, workflows, calendar, and opportunity pipeline remain in HighLevel.
