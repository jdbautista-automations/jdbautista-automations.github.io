About Me

Customer Support → Automation & Operations

I’m Joshua Bautista, an automation builder based in the Philippines with nearly seven years of customer-support experience across collections, food delivery, telecommunications, sales, technical support, and e-commerce.

That background gives me a practical view of the work surrounding customer service: monitoring conversations, maintaining records, handling exceptions, coordinating handoffs, protecting service levels, and keeping the customer informed.

I now combine that operational perspective with HighLevel, n8n, OpenAI, WhatsApp Cloud API, Airtable, Gmail, Google Sheets, Apps Script, JavaScript, Docker, Cloudflare, and API integrations to build systems around real operational work.

I’m also direct about implementation boundaries. If a connector or platform is new to me, I do not present it as past production experience; I scope it, work from the API and platform documentation, test it carefully, and document the resulting behavior.
