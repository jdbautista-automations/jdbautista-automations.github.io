01

02

AI-Enabled Operations

03

Keep identifiers, linked records, lifecycle state, and system authority consistent across connected tools.

How I Work

Operations first. Technology second.

My customer-support background shapes how I build: for the exception, the handoff, and the person who has to operate the system after deployment.

Start with operational reality

I map the actual journey, business rules, failure points, and human responsibilities before designing the workflow.

Separate understanding from authority

AI can interpret language. Deterministic rules and authoritative records should control transactions and lifecycle state.

Verify the resulting state

A green execution is not enough. I compare workflow history with the resulting contact, booking, opportunity, queue, or customer record.

My Approach

“Automate what is predictable. Use AI where understanding is required.”

ANNA proves the deterministic support foundation. Amihan places AI interpretation on top of guarded booking and payment state. Oakspan applies explicit control to multi-channel CRM intake, qualification, inspection scheduling, opportunity progression, and human handoff.
