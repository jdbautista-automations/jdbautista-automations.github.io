The Operational Problem

A roofing inquiry is only useful when the next action is clear.

Oakspan Roofing & Exteriors is a fictional residential-roofing business created as a portfolio environment. It models the work behind a local contractor receiving website-form, website-chat, Facebook, Instagram, and Email inquiries while coordinating inspections through one CRM.

The risk is not simply a missed message. A weak intake process can create duplicate opportunities, misroute an uncertain ZIP, treat an existing customer as a new sale, ignore a no-follow-up instruction, or continue automation when a person should take over.

Oakspan uses explicit custom fields, tags, branches, ownership rules, tasks, notifications, pipeline stages, and calendar events to keep the next action visible. The system favors deterministic business rules over speculative automation.

Deterministic control before automated persuasion Allowed cases progress. Missing, uncertain, warranty, conversational, and low-confidence cases stop and route to human review with the reason preserved.

Workflow Architecture

One operating model, twelve focused workflows.

Instead of placing every responsibility inside one oversized automation, the system separates intake, qualification, service-area logic, opportunity control, lifecycle synchronization, channel routing, ownership, and human handoff.

01 · CAPTURE

Normalize inbound inquiries

Website form, live chat, Messenger, Instagram DM, and Email enter the same operating environment with channel context retained.

02 · DECIDE

Resolve qualification and service area

ZIP, residential authority, service type, intent, and follow-up permission determine progression, protection, or review.

03 · SYNCHRONIZE

Keep calendar and opportunity state aligned

Booking and appointment completion move the opportunity through the inspection lifecycle without creating duplicate deals.

04 · HAND OFF

Stop automation when judgment is needed

Review cases receive the fields, tags, ownership, task, and notification context needed for a person to continue safely.

Channel expansion

Email was added as the fifth verified entry point by reusing the same guarded handoff contract already used by conversational channels: stamp the origin, record the outcome, require review, preserve the reason, and leave the record in a queue a person can act on. The channel expanded without creating a separate email-only operating model.

Operational Safeguards

Automation that knows when to stop.

The strongest part of the system is not how many actions it can execute. It is the set of conditions that prevent the wrong action from executing automatically.

Duplicate control

Find before creating

Opportunity workflows search first and disallow multiple opportunities so repeat execution does not create another deal.

Consent control

Respect no-follow-up

A no-follow-up choice exits qualification, records a Not Qualified outcome, and applies opt-out protection.

Confidence control

Review uncertain cases

Missing authority, unknown service, incomplete permission, and ambiguous ZIP data generate a Needs Information route.

Human operations

Assign, task, and notify

Handoff cases can receive an owner, a dated review task, and an internal notification so the operational next step remains visible.

Channel fidelity

Preserve inquiry origin

Channel-specific fields preserve Website Form, Web Chat, Messenger, Instagram DM, Email, and calendar context instead of flattening every record into an indistinguishable lead.

Automation boundary

Route before sending

WF-12 verifies inbound Email classification and human-review routing without adding an automated outbound email sequence. The observed reply remains a deliberate operator action inside HighLevel.

Operational Workspace

The automation resolves into work a team can see.

Pipeline stages and Smart Lists translate workflow state into operational views: what is qualified, what needs service-area review, what requires a human, and where an inspection sits in the sales lifecycle.

Production Boundary

Verified as a portfolio system, not represented as a live roofing business.

Oakspan is a synthetic demonstration, not a contractor account. No real customer information, service request, payment, or roofing appointment should be submitted through the page.

Email is verified through the connected Oakspan Gmail account for inbound intake and operator reply. A custom sending domain and automated outbound email sequence are not represented as completed capabilities. WhatsApp also remains outside the verified channel set and would require separate business-number and account setup.

GitHub Pages provides the public demonstration layer. The embedded form, live-chat widget, CRM records, conversations, workflows, calendar, Smart Lists, and opportunity pipeline remain in HighLevel.

What is actually verified Twelve published workflows, five inquiry channels, qualification and service-area alternatives, duplicate protection, booking/completion sync, opportunity ownership, human handoff operations, the public WF-11 website-chat path, and WF-12 Email intake with two-way conversation evidence were tested with synthetic records.
