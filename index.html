About Me

Customer Support → Automation

I'm Joshua Bautista, a customer-support professional and business automation builder in the Philippines.

I started my career in customer support, working across collections, food delivery, telecommunications, sales, and technical support. That experience gave me a practical understanding of the repetitive administrative work that happens behind customer service.

I'm now combining that operational experience with workflow automation to build systems that reduce repetitive work, organize information, and keep business processes moving.

My approach is simple: understand the process first, automate what is predictable, and use AI where it genuinely adds value.

I'm interested in helping businesses automate repetitive customer-support, operations, data, and workflow processes so their teams can spend less time managing the process around the work — and more time doing the work itself.
