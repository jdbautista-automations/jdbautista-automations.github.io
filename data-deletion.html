These instructions cover records voluntarily submitted through interactive portfolio demonstrations or created during an arranged test.

How to Request Deletion

Tell me which demonstration you used and how to find the test record.

  1. Email jdbautista.automations@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Portfolio Data Deletion Request.
  3. Identify the relevant project: Oakspan, Amihan, or ANNA.
  4. Identify the channel or test path you used and provide the minimum information needed to locate the record.
  5. Include the approximate date of the interaction if you remember it.
Do not send sensitive information Never include passwords, one-time PINs, financial credentials, government identification documents, real payment information, or other sensitive data in a deletion request. The portfolio demonstrations are designed for synthetic test information only.

Project Lookup Details

What to include for each demonstration.

OAKSPAN

HighLevel form, live chat, Email, Messenger, or Instagram test

State which Oakspan channel you used. Include the synthetic name, email address, or other test identifier you entered, plus the approximate date of the interaction. If you used the live website chat, mention that it was the Oakspan case-study chat widget.

AMIHAN

WhatsApp booking-operations test

Include the WhatsApp number or identifier used with the demo, including country code where applicable, and the approximate interaction date. If available, you may also provide a synthetic booking or conversation reference, but it is not required.

ANNA

Controlled support-workflow test

ANNA does not expose a public intake interface on the case-study page. If a test record was created for you through an arranged demonstration, identify the email address or synthetic ticket reference used and the approximate test date.

Verification

A small amount of verification may be needed before deleting a record.

A deletion request may require limited verification to reduce the risk of deleting someone else's record. Verification will be limited to information reasonably related to the demonstration record being requested.

Do not send identity documents or account passwords. If the record cannot be located from the project, channel, test identifier, and approximate date, additional non-sensitive context may be requested.

What the Request Covers

Deletion applies to portfolio-controlled demonstration records that can be identified.

Depending on the project, this may include:

  • demo contact or guest records;
  • conversation messages and channel metadata;
  • synthetic qualification, booking, ticket, opportunity, or workflow state;
  • human-handoff and review fields;
  • test notes, workflow records, or logs associated with the identified interaction; and
  • other portfolio-controlled records reasonably linked to the request.

Some technical records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, legal obligations, or to record that a deletion request was completed. Third-party platforms may also retain data independently under their own policies and system-level retention rules.

Processing

Verified requests are normally handled within 30 days.

Once a request is sufficiently verified and the relevant record can be located, deletion will normally be completed within 30 days. Confirmation will be sent to the requesting email address when the portfolio-controlled deletion work is complete or if additional information is required.
