Privacy Policy

Joshua Bautista Automation Portfolio

This policy explains how information may be handled when someone browses this portfolio or voluntarily interacts with one of its demonstration systems.

Last updated · September 4, 2026

Scope

One portfolio, several clearly separated demonstrations.

This website is the professional automation portfolio of Joshua Bautista. Its case studies include synthetic or controlled demonstrations built to show workflow design, customer operations, verification, and human handoff.

The public case-study pages themselves are primarily informational. Some projects also expose an interactive test channel. Information is processed only when a visitor chooses to use one of those available demo interfaces or participates in an arranged project test.

Demo-data rule Use synthetic information only. Do not submit passwords, one-time PINs, financial credentials, government identification numbers, real payment details, confidential business information, medical information, or other highly sensitive personal data through any portfolio demonstration.

Project-Specific Handling

What each case study does—and does not—collect.

OAKSPAN · INTERACTIVE

HighLevel lead-operations demo

Oakspan includes a public HighLevel website form and live-chat widget. If you use them, HighLevel may process the name, contact details, message content, timestamps, channel information, and workflow state you voluntarily provide. Connected test channels may also involve Meta or the Oakspan demo Gmail account during controlled verification.

AMIHAN · CONTROLLED DEMO

WhatsApp booking-operations demo

Amihan may process a WhatsApp phone number or identifier, profile name, messages, timestamps, simulated booking details, conversation state, and voluntarily submitted demonstration payment-proof content when the WhatsApp test journey is used.

ANNA · PRESENTATION

Customer-support operations case study

The public ANNA case-study page does not expose a public customer-intake form or inbox. Its underlying Gmail, n8n, Google Sheets, and Apps Script system is demonstrated with controlled test records rather than public visitor submissions.

Use of Information

Demo information is used to operate, verify, and improve the showcased workflows.

Depending on the demonstration, voluntarily submitted information may be used to:

  • respond to a synthetic inquiry or continue a test conversation;
  • create or update a demonstration contact, conversation, booking, ticket, or opportunity record;
  • apply qualification, routing, scheduling, duplicate-protection, lifecycle, or human-handoff rules;
  • verify that workflow execution produced the intended operational state;
  • troubleshoot failures, document test results, and improve the demonstration; and
  • protect the portfolio systems against misuse or unauthorized access.

Personal information is not sold. The demonstrations are not presented as live businesses providing roofing, tourism, payment, or customer-support services to the public.

Service Providers

The demonstrations rely on third-party platforms.

Depending on the project and test path, information may be processed through services used to operate the portfolio demonstrations, including:

  • GitHub Pages for public portfolio hosting;
  • HighLevel for Oakspan CRM, forms, live chat, conversations, calendars, and workflows;
  • Meta platforms and WhatsApp Cloud API for supported messaging demonstrations;
  • Gmail and Google Workspace services for demonstration email and operational records;
  • n8n for workflow orchestration;
  • Airtable for Amihan demonstration records;
  • OpenAI API for Amihan language interpretation;
  • Cloudflare for Amihan test connectivity; and
  • Docker-hosted services used in controlled workflow environments.

These providers may handle information under their own terms, security practices, and retention rules. This portfolio policy describes the project owner's handling of demonstration information; it does not replace a provider's own privacy policy.

Retention & Security

Test records are kept only as long as reasonably useful for the demonstration.

Demonstration records may be retained for portfolio verification, troubleshooting, regression testing, security, and documentation, and may be removed during test resets. Reasonable administrative and technical controls are used to restrict access to connected systems and credentials.

No online transmission or storage platform can be guaranteed completely secure. Visitors should therefore follow the synthetic-data rule and avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information.

Requests & Contact

You can ask about or request deletion of a demo record.

To request deletion of information associated with a portfolio demonstration, follow the User Data Deletion instructions. The request should identify the relevant project and test channel without including passwords or sensitive documents.

Questions about this policy may be sent to jdbautista.automations@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when the portfolio, its interactive demos, or connected services materially change. The revision date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
