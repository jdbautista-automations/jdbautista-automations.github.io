How I Work

Operations first. Technology second.

My customer-support background shapes how I build: for the exception, the handoff, and the person who has to operate the system after deployment.

Start with operational reality

I map the actual journey, business rules, failure points, and human responsibilities before designing the workflow.

Separate understanding from authority

AI can interpret language. Deterministic rules and authoritative records should control transactions and lifecycle state.

Test beyond the happy path

Duplicate requests, repeated decisions, cross-period records, late replies, and post-completion messages reveal the real system.

My Approach

“Automate what is predictable. Use AI where understanding is required.”

ANNA proves the deterministic operational foundation. Amihan shows how AI interpretation can sit on top of guarded booking and payment state. Oakspan applies the same control philosophy to multi-channel CRM lead intake, qualification, human handoff, scheduling, and opportunity progression.

About Me

I’m Joshua Bautista, an automation builder based in the Philippines with nearly seven years of customer-support experience across collections, food delivery, telecommunications, sales, technical support, and e-commerce.

That background gives me a practical view of the administrative work around customer service: monitoring conversations, maintaining records, handling exceptions, coordinating handoffs, and keeping the customer informed.

I now combine that operational perspective with HighLevel, n8n, OpenAI, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Airtable, Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, JavaScript, Docker, and API integrations to build systems around real work—not simply connect one application to another.

My goal is to help teams spend less time managing the process around their work and more time resolving customer and business needs. I work best in clear, documentation-led environments where progress, decisions, limitations, and next steps remain visible across time zones.
