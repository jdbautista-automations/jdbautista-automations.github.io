Why Amihan Exists

A delayed reply can quietly become a lost booking.

A guest rarely experiences tourism operations as a clean sequence of database actions. They ask conversational questions, change details, repeat themselves, send payment proof, and expect someone to respond before they continue searching elsewhere.

Amihan is inspired by the fictional Amihan Coastal Lodge: a small tourism business where missed or delayed WhatsApp inquiries can mean lost revenue, while rushed manual handling can create duplicate reservations, oversold dates, incomplete records, or unverified payment confirmations.

The system connects a real WhatsApp Business number to self-hosted n8n, OpenAI, and a linked Airtable model. It responds quickly to the guest while preserving explicit authority over inventory, bookings, payment state, and the employee decision.

Understanding is not authority OpenAI interprets the guest’s language. n8n applies the workflow rules; Airtable remains the operational source of truth; and an employee—not the model—approves or rejects submitted payment proof. The core journey is live MVP verified. Permanent hosting and the designed v5.2 post-completion router remain future work.

Operating Model

AI interprets. Operational records decide.

The guest-facing workflow handles conversation and transaction orchestration. A separate Airtable-triggered admin workflow processes the employee’s payment decision and returns the correct result to the guest.

01 · Receive

Meta webhook

Messages from a real WhatsApp user arrive through the verified Meta webhook.

02 · Resolve

Guest and context

n8n normalizes the payload and loads or creates the linked guest and conversation.

03 · Understand

Intent and details

OpenAI extracts the request and relevant booking details from natural language.

04 · Verify

Airtable state

Offerings, dates, capacity, existing bookings, settings, and conversation state are checked.

05 · Decide

Guarded branches

Deterministic rules control reservation, duplicate protection, payment, and handoff behavior.

06 · Complete

Guest or staff action

WhatsApp sends the response, or the admin workflow waits for and processes employee review.

Verified Guest Journey

From availability question to controlled confirmation.

The live demonstration followed one coherent transaction path, including the repeat actions that commonly expose weak booking and payment workflows.

01 · INQUIRY

Understand the request

The guest asks naturally. Amihan resolves the person and conversation, checks the requested dates, and returns verified availability.

02 · RESERVATION

Create a pending booking

A final capacity check reserves the units and creates a linked booking with a unique reference and Pending / Unpaid state.

03 · DUPLICATE GUARD

Reuse the existing record

If the same request is repeated, the workflow returns the linked booking instead of reserving inventory or creating another record.

04 · PAYMENT

Send configured instructions

Business Settings supply the method, account details, amount, booking reference, and demonstration warnings.

05 · HANDOFF

Hold state during review

Submitted proof moves the conversation to Payment Verification / Human Handoff while the booking remains Pending / Unpaid.

06 · DECISION

Let the employee decide

Approval produces Confirmed / Deposit Paid; rejection preserves Pending / Unpaid and returns the reviewer’s correction note.

07 · REPEAT SAFETY

Process the decision once

A processed flag and state checks prevent a repeated approval or rejection from changing records or notifying the guest twice.

Operational Data & Human Authority

Business configuration and transaction state stay connected.

Business-specific values live in Airtable rather than inside the automation. That keeps the workflow reusable while preserving linked records from the first guest message through the employee’s decision.

Business Settings

Payment configuration, currency, instructions, messaging, and demonstration controls.

Offerings

The accommodation or tour catalog, pricing, capacity, amenities, and active status.

Availability

Date-level units, rates, status, and links to the bookings using that inventory.

Guests

WhatsApp identity, contact details, and relationships to conversations and reservations.

Bookings

Dates, party size, totals, booking status, payment status, and linked operational records.

Conversations

Latest messages, stage, extracted intent, summary, handoff state, and review fields.

FAQs

Reusable business answers and policy information available to guest responses.

Payment proof does not equal payment approval Receiving proof changes the conversation to a staff-review stage but does not confirm the booking. Only the employee’s Airtable decision authorizes the final booking and payment-state update.

The designed v5.2 router will check completed state first: courtesy messages close cleanly; explicit new bookings begin a new journey; support, modification, or cancellation routes to the correct workflow or staff member; and ambiguous messages receive one clarifying question without reopening availability.
