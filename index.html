Featured Project

ANNA v2

Automated Customer Support Ticketing System
Production verified · v2.3.2

ANNA v2.3.2 is a self-hosted, conversation-aware customer-support ticketing and operations system. It converts incoming Gmail conversations into structured tickets, routes requests, maintains lifecycle and SLA data, and gives agents practical working queues in Google Sheets.

The system is deterministic-first: predictable work is handled through explicit business rules, while the architecture remains ready for an AI intelligence layer in ANNA v3. ANNA v2 is AI-ready, but it is not presented as AI-powered.

Production Capabilities

More than Gmail connected to a spreadsheet.

ANNA maintains conversation state, enforces ticket lifecycle rules, synchronizes operational data, and presents agent-facing workspaces.

01

Conversation-aware ticketing

Gmail Thread IDs and a global Conversation_Index distinguish new requests from replies and prevent duplicate ticket creation across monthly ticket databases.

02

Rules-based routing and intake

Incoming messages are processed chronologically, routed to Support, Billing, Sales, or Manual Review, labeled in Gmail, and registered with structured Ticket IDs.

03

Agent operations

TODAY'S QUEUE and FOLLOW_UP provide editable operational views with clickable Gmail-linked Ticket IDs, controlled assignments, statuses, and Last Agent Reply tracking.

04

Lifecycle, SLA, and after-hours handling

Guarded status transitions, reopen logic, business-calendar duration, pending-time tracking, acknowledgements, cooldown rules, and automatic closure are handled consistently.

05

Customer and management data

Customer Master aggregates ticket history, while the client-facing Dashboard reports operational status, department, priority, and aging information from the active reporting month.

06

Reliability and traceability

Locking, schema guardrails, audit records, cross-sheet resolution, settings-driven scheduling, and persistent Docker storage protect the system from common operational failures.

Current Architecture

Deterministic automation from inbox to agent action.

n8n handles scheduled intake and workflow orchestration. Google Apps Script enforces the operational rules around the Sheets-based ticketing layer.

Intake

Gmail + n8n

Settings-driven business-hours and after-hours schedulers fetch unread messages in controlled batches and normalize them for processing.

Operations

Apps Script + Sheets

Ticket lifecycle, cross-month lookup, customer synchronization, agent edits, maintenance, audit logging, and reporting are managed here.

Human work

Queues + Gmail threads

Agents work from prioritized operational views and open the authoritative Gmail conversation directly through each linked Ticket ID.
