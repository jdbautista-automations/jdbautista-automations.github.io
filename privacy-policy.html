Privacy Policy

Amihan Booking Bot Demo
Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JDBautista Automations collects, uses, stores, and handles information processed through the Amihan Booking Bot Demo.

Demo notice: Amihan Booking Bot Demo is a portfolio and testing project. It is not currently operating as a real tourism business. Payment details displayed by the bot are fictional. Do not send real money, passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information through the demo.

1. Information We Process

The demo may process the following information:

  • WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp ID, and profile name;
  • Messages sent to and received from the bot;
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery information;
  • Booking details such as selected offering, dates, number of adults and children, booking status, and payment status;
  • Payment-proof messages or media voluntarily submitted for simulated manual review; and
  • Technical logs used for testing, security, debugging, and workflow improvement.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries through WhatsApp;
  • Check simulated availability and prepare booking records;
  • Prevent duplicate reservations;
  • Provide fictional demo payment instructions;
  • Route payment-proof submissions for manual review;
  • Maintain conversation and booking history;
  • Troubleshoot errors and improve the automation; and
  • Protect the demo against misuse or unauthorized access.

3. Services Used to Process Information

The demo may process information through service providers needed to operate the automation, including:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for messaging and webhook delivery;
  • n8n for workflow automation;
  • Airtable for demo booking and conversation records;
  • OpenAI API for message understanding and response generation; and
  • Cloudflare for secure public webhook connectivity.

These providers may process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for demonstration, testing, troubleshooting, security, and portfolio evaluation. Demo records may also be removed during testing resets.

5. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information, including restricted credentials and authenticated access to connected services. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Deletion Requests

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with your WhatsApp number by emailing jdbautista.automations@gmail.com .

Include the WhatsApp number used with the demo and state that the request concerns Amihan Booking Bot Demo. Do not include passwords, payment credentials, or identification documents.

We may ask for limited information to verify that the requester controls the relevant WhatsApp number. Verified deletion requests will normally be processed within 30 days, unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Detailed instructions are available on our User Data Deletion page.

7. Children's Privacy

The demo is intended for use by adults. Booking details may include the number of child guests, but children should not directly submit personal information through the demo.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the demo, connected services, or data-handling practices change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
